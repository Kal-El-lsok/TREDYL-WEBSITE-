OFFICIAL SCHOOL RESULTS ANNOUNCEMENT

The Management of TREDYL International Schools is pleased to inform all students, parents, and guardians that the official results for the 3rd term examination will be released on 11 December 2025.

All results will be accessible through our official online portal. Students are advised to have their roll numbers ready for access.

The results release will follow this schedule:

  • 9:00 AM: Primary Section Results
  • 11:00 AM: Junior Secondary Results
  • 2:00 PM: Senior Secondary Results

For any technical issues accessing results, please contact the ICT department at the school premises.
