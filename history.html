OUR PROUD HISTORY

Journey Through Time: From Dream to Reality

THE ROOT OF OUR GREAT SCHOOL

A Journey of Vision, Dedication, and Growth

2002

The Dream Begins

The dream of establishing this school came about eleven (11) years ago when the proprietress of the school, MRS. REGINA ALAS GIRMA, sold the idea of building a school to her husband, the director of the school MR. ALAS TARU GIRMA.

He accepted the idea without any delay, though the realization of the dream and the kick-off came in September, 2013.

2013

The School Opens

The school officially opened in September 2013 and was dedicated in December, 2013 by REV. JAMES DABO MOORE, who is named in one of the houses in the School.
