ADMISSION PROCESS

Join Our Community of Excellence

APPLY FOR ADMISSION FROM ANYWHERE

Apply for admission from anywhere around the world with ease using our online application system.

1

Obtain Form

Prospective students are to obtain the school's admission form online, or in the school's administrative office.

2

Entrance Exam

Look out for the dates of entrance examination which will be executed before selection.

3

Admission Letter

Admission letters for successful candidates will be made available in the candidate's email or the school administrative office.

4

Fee Payment

Every successful candidate will be required to pay tuition and other spelt out fees which will be attached to the admission letter.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION

Prospective students will be required to submit the following for registration and documentation purpose:

Birth Certificate

A photocopy of birth certificate

State of Origin

A photocopy of certificate of state of origin

Passport Photographs

Two recent passport photocopy

Medical Records

Certificate of medical fitness or record of any medical history that requires special attention

Previous Results

Last result from previous school for students on transfer
