WELCOME!

Welcome to TREDYL INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL, where our aim is to provide the very best for every child within a caring environment where everyone is happy, safe and secure.

Within each classroom, students are encouraged to pursue their interest in a safe, welcoming environment.

Because we offer students a supportive and future oriented learning environment sustained by quality teachers, experienced staff and modern school facilities.
